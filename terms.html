Each project is defined by the proposal, quote, or written agreement provided before work begins. That document outlines what's included, timelines, and pricing. Work outside that scope, additional pages, features, or services not originally agreed to, may require a separate quote.

Payment

  • A deposit is typically required before work begins, with the balance due at project completion or per an agreed payment schedule.

Projects include a reasonable number of revision rounds as outlined in your proposal. Requests significantly beyond the original scope or repeated large-scale changes after approval may be billed separately.

Cancellation

Either party may end an ongoing engagement with written notice as outlined in your specific agreement. Work completed up to that point is billable. Deposits for one-time projects are generally non-refundable once work has begun, given the time already committed.
