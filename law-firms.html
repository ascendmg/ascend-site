Inquiries to Qualified

Inquiries → Qualified Inquiries

What the Data Shows

36 of 60 inquiries (60%) fit the firm’s criteria. The 2,000 website sessions that month produced those 60 inquiries, a 3% inquiry rate.
36 ÷ 60 = 60% qualified

What We Would Investigate

  • What the unqualified inquiries ask about
  • Whether practice areas, locations and languages are clear on the site
  • Whether the phone number is one tap away on mobile
  • Which sources and pages produce qualified inquiries

How HQAscend Could Help

  • Clearer practice area pages and scope statements
  • Simple contact options on every page, including click-to-call
  • Contact forms that ask the right first questions
  • Source tracking for calls and forms

Qualified to Consultation

Qualified Inquiries → Booked Consultations

What the Data Shows

18 of 36 qualified inquiries (50%) booked a consultation.
18 ÷ 36 = 50% booked

What We Would Investigate

  • How quickly the office responds
  • Whether visitors can see what happens next
  • Whether missed calls and form replies are followed up
  • Handoff between the website and the firm’s intake process

How HQAscend Could Help

  • Clear next-step messaging and consultation request options
  • Confirmation messages and follow-up emails
  • Tracking that shows which inquiries are still waiting for a reply, using the firm’s own intake tools

Consultation to Client

Booked Consultations → Retained Clients

What the Data Shows

9 of 18 consultations (50%) became retained clients. This step depends mostly on the consultation itself, the matter and the client’s decision.
9 ÷ 18 = 50% retained

What We Would Investigate

  • Whether people arrive with the right expectations about fees and process
  • Which sources bring clients who go on to retain the firm
  • Whether follow-up after consultations is consistent

How HQAscend Could Help

  • Pages and emails that set accurate expectations before the consultation
  • Reporting that connects source to retained client
  • Honest content about the process, with no promises about outcomes
