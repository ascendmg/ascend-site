Sales page

Visits → checkout starts

What the data shows

200 of 4,000 sales-page visits (5%) started checkout.
200 ÷ 4,000 = 5% checkout start rate

What we would investigate

  • Whether the page shows what students learn, who it is for, the price and how long access lasts
  • Whether the lesson preview is used
  • How the page reads on a phone
  • Which traffic sources send visitors who start checkout

How HQAscend could help

  • A clear curriculum and a useful lesson preview
  • Straightforward pricing and access information
  • Answers to common questions near the buy button
  • Source tracking

Checkout to enrollment

Checkout starts → enrollments

What the data shows

120 of 200 checkout starts (60%) became enrollments. The data shows where people stopped, not why.
120 ÷ 200 = 60% completed

What we would investigate

  • Whether the total price or any fee is a surprise
  • Whether creating an account adds friction
  • Payment errors, especially on mobile
  • Whether the confirmation and first-login path are clear

How HQAscend could help

  • Clearer pricing and access details before payment
  • Checkout set up within the platform’s capabilities
  • A confirmation email and a simple path to the first lesson
  • Tracking of each checkout step

First lesson

Enrolled → started lesson 1

What the data shows

96 of 120 enrolled students (80%) opened their first lesson within 14 days. 24 never began.
96 ÷ 120 = 80%

What we would investigate

  • Whether students receive a clear welcome and login instructions
  • How easy it is to find the first lesson on a phone
  • Whether the dashboard shows a clear next step

How HQAscend could help

  • A welcome email with a direct link
  • A clear dashboard with “start here” and “next lesson” buttons
  • Accessible navigation and mobile layouts

First module

Started → finished module 1

What the data shows

72 of the 96 students who started (75%) finished module 1. That is 60% of everyone enrolled.
72 ÷ 96 = 75% of starters · 72 ÷ 120 = 60% of enrolled

What we would investigate

  • Where in module 1 students stop
  • Whether lessons are the right length
  • Whether progress is visible

How HQAscend could help

  • Visible progress and lesson order
  • Short, clearly labeled lessons
  • Tracking at lesson level, within the platform’s capabilities

Ongoing progress

Finished module 1 → active on days 8–14

What the data shows

36 of the 72 students who finished module 1 (50%) completed a lesson on days 8–14. That is 30% of everyone enrolled.
36 ÷ 72 = 50% of module-1 finishers · 36 ÷ 120 = 30% of enrolled

What we would investigate

  • Whether students get a reminder or a reason to return
  • Whether the next module is easy to find
  • Whether the course content and pacing suit the audience

How HQAscend could help

  • Progress reminder emails
  • A “continue where you left off” button
  • Reporting on active students each week
