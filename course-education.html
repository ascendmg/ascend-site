What you will learn

Four skills that make a loaf repeatable

What a site like this could track

  • Checkout start rateOf the people who visit the sales page, how many start checkout. Needs analytics set up on a real site.
  • Lesson preview engagementHow many visitors open the free lesson.
  • Curriculum opensHow often visitors open a module. A sign they are checking whether the course covers what they need.
  • Checkout completionOf the people who start checkout in this preview, how many reach the confirmation screen.
  • Successful enrollmentsHow many previews reach confirmation. On a real site this is the number of confirmed purchases.
  • Lesson startsHow many lessons students open.
  • First-lesson completionHow many students finish a lesson once they start.
  • Returning learnersHow often students come back to the dashboard.
  • Resume clicksHow often students use the resume button.
  • Module opensHow often students open a module from the dashboard.

What the data shows

200 of 4,000 sales-page visits (5%) started checkout.
200 ÷ 4,000 = 5% checkout start rate

What we would investigate

  • Whether the page shows what students learn, who it is for, the price and how long access lasts
  • Whether the lesson preview is used
  • How the page reads on a phone
  • Which traffic sources send visitors who start checkout
