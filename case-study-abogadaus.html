Case Study · Law Firm

AbogadaUS.com

A bilingual website for the Law Offices of Ingrid Morfa, covering immigration, family, criminal, and social security disability cases.

Visit Site ↗
AbogadaUS
Goal

A trustworthy online presence for a local practice.

For a law firm, the site's main job is convincing a stranger to trust you with something serious, before they've ever spoken to you. It needed to give the practice a clear, credible presence and make it effortless for prospective clients to reach the office directly.

Approach

Practice area clarity, in two languages.

Dedicated pages for each practice area (immigration, disability, and more) so visitors immediately know if the firm handles their situation. Bilingual content runs throughout, and a direct contact form plus click-to-call phone number sit on every page.

Practice Area PagesBilingual ContentClick-to-Call
Result

A real case study on the SEO side, too.

AbogadaUS is also the first site fully audited by Ascend's own SEO system, real findings, real recommendations, not a demo. It scored 88/100 overall with a handful of concrete, fixable issues identified, like missing meta descriptions and a couple of thin pages, exactly the kind of ongoing work Local and Growth SEO plans are built to handle.
