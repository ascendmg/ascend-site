Privacy Policy

How we handle your information.

Last updated August 2026

Ascend ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect through hqascend.com and how it's used.

What we collect

When you submit the contact form on this site, we collect the information you provide: your name, email address, phone number (if given), and message. We don't use tracking cookies or collect any information beyond what you submit directly.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and follow up about your project
  • To keep a record of client communication

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Email delivery

Contact form submissions are sent using Resend, a transactional email service, solely to deliver your message to us. Resend does not use this information for its own purposes.

Your rights

You can request that we delete any information you've submitted at any time by emailing michael@hqascend.com.

Questions

If you have any questions about this policy, reach out at michael@hqascend.com.
