Privacy Policy

How We Handle Your Information.

Last updated October 2026

HQAscend ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This page explains what information is collected through hqascend.com, which outside services handle it, and how it is used.

Information You Give Us

When you submit the contact form, we receive the details you enter: your name, email address, phone number (if given), and message. This site does not take payments. Clients who agree to work with us pay through a private payment link or invoice from Stripe, where card details go directly to Stripe and we never see or store your card number. If you book a call, the details you enter go to Calendly.

Information Collected Automatically

This site uses Vercel Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used. Vercel describes this service as measuring page views, referring sites, and general device, browser, and country information, without using cookies to follow you across websites. Our hosting provider, Vercel, also keeps standard server logs, which can include your IP address and browser details, for security and reliability. We use this information in aggregate to improve the site, not to build profiles of individual visitors.

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers to fetch them, and Google receives your IP address as part of that request. Some Learn guides include checklists that save your progress in your own browser (local storage). That data stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Cookies

We have not set up advertising or marketing cookies on this site. Vercel Web Analytics is described by Vercel as cookie-free. Outside services you choose to use from this site, such as Calendly booking or a Stripe payment link or invoice we send you, may set their own cookies under their own policies.

How We Use It

  • To respond to your inquiry and follow up about your project
  • To keep a record of client communication
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the site is used and keep it secure

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

Service Providers

We rely on these providers to run the site. Each handles information under its own privacy policy:

  • Vercel hosts the site and provides Web Analytics.
  • Resend delivers contact form messages to our inbox.
  • Stripe processes payments when you pay a private payment link or invoice from us after an agreement.
  • Calendly handles consultation bookings.
  • Google Fonts serves the site's typefaces.

Your Rights

You can ask us to access or delete any information you've submitted by emailing michael@hqascend.com. For information held by the providers above, you may also need to contact them directly.

Questions

If you have any questions about this policy, reach out at michael@hqascend.com.
