What happens when

A yard has a different job each season. Here is how a year usually runs.

Spring in Central Florida

  • Weekly mowing begins as grass wakes up
  • Bed clean-out and fresh mulch
  • Prune spring-flowering shrubs after they bloom

Summer in Central Florida

  • Weekly mowing and edging
  • Hedge shaping once growth slows
  • Storm-prep walk-through before hurricane season peaks

Fall in Central Florida

  • Leaf clean-ups under oaks, often two or three visits
  • Best planting season for new beds
  • Fresh mulch before the cool months

Winter in Central Florida

  • Light monthly visits while grass is dormant
  • Pruning of select shrubs and small trees
  • Plan next year's beds and trimming dates

Tree and shrub trimming

Shaping, thinning and clean-up that keep hedges and shrubs healthy and in bounds.

What is included

  • Hedge shaping and size reduction
  • Thinning so light and air reach the center
  • Pruning at the right time for each plant
  • Hand-cut corners and clean lines
  • Every cutting hauled away

What needs trimming?

Hedges

Shaped with the base slightly wider than the top so lower leaves keep their light. Done once or twice a year, depending on the plant.

Each visitShape, thin, haul away

Shrubs

Pruned to keep their own form. We remove crowded or crossing stems and cut flowering shrubs after they bloom.

Each visitThin, shape, haul away

Small trees

Lifting low branches off walks and roofs and removing dead wood on small trees. Larger trees need a certified arborist, and we will say so.

Each visitLift, thin, haul away

What a visit looks like

  1. 1

    Look it over

    We check the plants and decide what to cut and what to leave.

  2. 2

    Trim and shape

    Cuts are made at the right time for each plant, not all at once.

  3. 3

    Haul away

    Cuttings are bagged or hauled. The beds are left clean.
