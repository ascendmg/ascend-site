Inquiries to qualified

Inquiries → qualified requests

What the data shows

70 of 100 inquiries (70%) were in the service area and for a service the business offers. The other 30 were outside one or both.
70 ÷ 100 = 70% qualified

What we would investigate

  • Which areas and services the unqualified requests mention
  • Whether the service list and coverage area are easy to find
  • Whether the call button is visible on a phone

How HQAscend could help

  • A clear services list and service-area page
  • Mobile contact options: tap to call, tap to text, short request form
  • Inquiry tracking by source and page

Qualified requests → quotes or appointments

What the data shows

42 of 70 qualified requests (60%) received a quote or booked an appointment. Some businesses book directly; others quote first.
42 ÷ 70 = 60%

What we would investigate

  • How quickly requests get a first reply
  • Whether missed calls and form requests are followed up
  • Whether the request form collects what is needed, such as address or a photo

How HQAscend could help

  • A quote request or booking option that suits how the business works
  • Confirmation messages and follow-up templates
  • Tracking that shows which requests are still waiting for a reply

What the data shows

21 of 42 quotes or appointments (50%) turned into completed jobs.
21 ÷ 42 = 50% completed

What we would investigate

  • Whether pricing, timing and availability matched what customers expected
  • How quickly quotes are delivered
  • Which sources lead to completed jobs, not just inquiries

How HQAscend could help

  • Clear information about services, process and timing before the quote
  • Reporting from source to completed job, using the business’s own records
  • Reviews and photos of real work, when they are authentic and approved
