Complex Process. Simple Experience.

The Challenge

Create an approachable, bilingual learning platform for people preparing for U.S. citizenship, including users who may not be comfortable with technology.

  • Unfamiliar forms and steps
  • English-only study material
  • Confusing checkout
Our Solution

Build a guided, mobile-friendly learning experience with clear navigation, organized lessons, interactive practice tools, and a streamlined enrollment flow.

  • Seven clear stages
  • English & Spanish
  • One simple purchase

Guided Course Structure

Seven stages, from Welcome to the Oath Ceremony.

Each stage covers one part of the process so learners always know what comes next.

Bilingual Interface

English and Spanish, one tap apart.

A language switch sits in the header, so learners can move between English and Spanish support.

Interactive Practice

Civics practice, reading & writing, mock interview prep.

Practice with instant feedback, plus progress tracking to show what needs more study.
